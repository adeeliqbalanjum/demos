Do I have to pay now?

No. Reserve your seats and pay the driver in cash when he arrives, or pay by card online if you prefer. No card is needed to hold a booking.

Where do you pick up from?

From any hotel or address in Dubai. Pickup from Sharjah is on request. Tell us the place when you reserve, or later on WhatsApp.

How do I get my ticket?

On WhatsApp, within minutes of reserving: your voucher, the pickup time and your driver's contact.

Can I change the date?

Send one WhatsApp message with the new date and we move the booking. Cancellation terms are shown on each tour.
