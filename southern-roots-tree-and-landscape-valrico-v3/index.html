Questions

Before you call

Do I need a permit to remove a tree?+

Many cities and counties require one for larger trees. We tell you if yours does and handle the paperwork.

Are you insured?+

Yes, liability and workers comp. Ask and we send the certificate before the job.

Can you remove a tree close to the house?+

Yes. Sectional take-downs with ropes or a crane are how we handle tight spots.

Do you grind the stump too?+

Yes, as an option on any removal. Chips can be left as mulch or hauled away.

Do you give free quotes?+

Yes. We look at the job, then give you a written price. No charge and no obligation.
